## About

**THREWS ODYSSEY 2026** is an online **research and innovation** hackathon run by Threws – The Research World. It brings together students, developers, researchers and technology enthusiasts to build practical solutions to real-world problems.

- **Category:** Applied Research
- **Mode:** Online
- **Team size:** 1–4
- **Registration:** Closed 30 Oct 2026, 2:30 PM EDT (~12:00 AM IST, 31 Oct). 662 registered.
- **Prize pool:** ₹1,50,000 cash + participation certificates

**What to build:** Find a meaningful problem and build a working solution. Projects can be software products, AI/ML systems, research prototypes, cybersecurity tools, and so on.

### Round 1 — Project Submission (on Unstop)
Submit before the deadline:
- Project details
- Problem statement
- Proposed solution
- Technology stack
- Source code / repository
- Working demo (where applicable)

### Round 2 — Final Presentation & Demo (online)
Shortlisted teams present to the THREWS ODYSSEY judging panel:
- Live demo of the solution
- Explain the problem and technical approach
- **Discuss research and experimentation**
- Q&A with judges

Winners are chosen on the overall evaluation plus the final presentation.
