How it works

  1. You pay as usual and say what it's for, by voice or text.
  2. Pauseline looks for signs of a scam. If unsure, it asks one or two yes or no questions.
  3. PayPal holds the money. Nothing is taken yet.
  4. Normal payments go at once. Risky ones wait for {{ contact_name }}, or for a cooling-off period. If declined, you are never charged.
